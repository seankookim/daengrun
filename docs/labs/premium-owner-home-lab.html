Owner home — how much of the screen does the current state get?

Every number, name and time on this page is SAMPLE data. None of it comes from a server and none of it ships. Every element is bound to a field that actually exists in owner/home.tsx and home-hero.tsx. Photo positions are drawn as labelled empty slots rather than stock images, for the reason in docs/design/premium-taste-brief.md § Tension ⑧. Korean is the only language inside the phone frames, because that is the copy a user reads; everything outside a frame is English, per CLAUDE.md § Language.
  • The pick: what SHAPE the current state takes at the top of the screen. The masthead, the three chunks (오늘 · 동네 · 나) and the dock are identical in ①②③ and are not part of the pick.
  • Reference state: confirmed — a runner is booked, the run has not started. Each variant carries a state table showing how the same grammar handles none · searching · handoff · active. A variant that cannot carry all five is out.
  • ① and ② stay inside the paper world. ③ deliberately leaves it and is flagged as a departure, with the exact DESIGN.md rules it breaks printed beside it. Sean's question was "are you continuing the paper style?" — the answer is yes for ①②, no for ③, on purpose.
  • Dials: DESIGN_VARIANCE / MOTION_INTENSITY / VISUAL_DENSITY. The brief's §(d) recommendation for owner home is 4 / 3 / 5.

How to choose

  1. ①Refine what ships · 3 / 2 / 5 · paper world. Chip, 43pt phrase over two lines, subline, three action blocks. Same structure as trunk; only spacing and hierarchy move.
  2. ②One decision card · 4 / 3 / 4 · paper world. State and action bind into one ink-bordered card that says one thing, then acts. Secondary actions demote to rows.
  3. ③DEPARTURE — editorial soft card · 6 / 3 / 3 · outside the paper world. 16px radii, ambient shadow, pill buttons, no coral hairline. This is what impeccable's craft floor plus soft-skill's premium grammar actually produce when DESIGN.md's paper constraints are relaxed.

How to answer: by number. Mixing is fine — "②, but the dark band from ③". A disagreement in one line is the most useful reply.

What this variant breaks, named
  • DESIGN.md §2 / §3b — "Cards: radius 0" and "Radius 0 everywhere including the club card". Here every surface is 16px.
  • DESIGN.md §2 — "Soft shadows retire with the rounded corners (shadow only where a floating surface genuinely floats)". Here every card floats.
  • DESIGN.md §2 — "Hairline = solid coral #E8552F 1px, full-bleed … The line is the brand." There is no coral line on this screen at all. This is the largest single loss.
  • DESIGN.md §3b — the button matrix ("All: radius 0") and the 4px pressed-fill lip, replaced by a pill with a tinted shadow. §3b also says "a drop shadow and a lip are two different depth languages and must not sit on one control"; this variant chooses the shadow and drops the lip entirely.
  • DESIGN.md §4 — "Section division by full-bleed hairline, not by cards. Card-soup and rounded card grids are retired". This is three rounded cards.
  • DESIGN.md header — the style freeze: "no NEW aesthetics until 50 paying dogs". This is a new aesthetic.
What it still obeys, and why that matters. White ground, Korean at 15pt or above, real fields only, no gradient text, no glass or blur, no emoji as icons, no AI-beige, every transition under 240ms, reduced motion honoured, WCAG AA contrast. A departure from the paper world is not a departure from the house laws, and the difference is the whole point of drawing it. soft-skill §4: "Never place a premium card, image, or container flatly on the background … nested enclosures", plus "exaggerated squircle radii (rounded-[2rem])" and "Primary interactive buttons must be fully rounded pills". This variant takes the intent (concentric physical hierarchy) at half the prescribed radius, and refuses the double-bezel outright — Sean's "clean look without cards within cards" survives even here. taste-skill §4.4 "SHAPE CONSISTENCY LOCK (mandatory): Pick ONE corner-radius scale for the page and stick to it." One scale: 16px surfaces, 999px controls, and a documented rule rather than a mixture. The honest argument against it. The coral hairline is the one mark that makes a daengrun screen recognisable at a glance; every competitor's app already looks like this variant. taste-skill's own §4.2 warns that when a brand reaches for the category default "the brand becomes invisible". Removing the line to gain softness trades identity for familiarity, and this product's identity is doing real work.

Three faces. Loading is a named Korean sentence, failure is the critical strip with a working retry, empty is a sentence with the next action attached. The moment any two of them look the same, this house calls it a defect. impeccable operate.md: "Empty states that teach the interface, not 'nothing here.'" · taste-skill §4.5 requires the full cycle: loading, empty, error. docs/design/loading-state-audit.md measured 46 of 59 routes naming their loading state in a Korean sentence and zero production spinners. The house idiom is a sentence, so these labs use sentences.

What this lab deliberately did not do

1. Not one photograph. taste-skill §4.8 calls a text-only page "slop" and asks for at least a picsum.photos placeholder. This lab refuses. The product's whole claim is that a particular run really happened; putting a stranger's dog into the mock breaks that claim in the mock itself. The conflict and its real cost are written up as § Tension ⑧ in the brief. The cost is genuine: a lab with no photographs cannot answer "does this composition survive a real photo", and only a device smoke can. 2. Every dial sits below taste-skill's baseline. Its default is 8 / 6 / 4; the highest value here is 6 / 3 / 5. taste-skill §0.A: "Quiet constraints … OVERRIDE aesthetic preference." A product where a stranger takes your dog is trust-first, and the style freeze is the outer gate. To go bolder the lever is the freeze, not the dial. 3. Zero side-stripe borders. That is the exact rule (side-tab) impeccable's own detector fired on in seven of this repo's existing labs. Check it: grep -cE "border-(left|right): *[2-9]" against this file should return 0. 4. A paper-world artifact stub was drawn and dropped. A boarding-pass stub for the confirmed booking fits DESIGN.md §1's artifact grammar and §4's "a card must BE the interaction". It was cut because §8 budgets the artifact: owner/meetup already owns the ticket and shot/[bid] owns the card, and a third screen using the same metaphor makes one of them a costume.
