the thing that keeps it from becoming ten identical slabs

Drawn buttons are rationed by tier

If every module were a 90px drawn button the screen would read as a wall of cards — the exact density problem the ⑧ v2 grammar was built to escape. So the drawing system is graded:

TIER 2Full drawn buttons, 90pt — 지금 찾기 · 미리 예약. The decisions. Coral fill on the primary only.
TIER 3Native surfaces, no button chrome — the club widget (engraved), the runner strip, the course tiles. These are browsing, and browsing wants real content, not labels.
TIER 4Short drawn rows, 64pt — 하이 포인트 · 자랑하기 · 안심 센터. Same vocabulary at two-thirds height, so 나 reads as a coda rather than a second act.
DATANo button at all — the record row (이번 주 · 통산 · 도장) is Oswald numerals under a 2px rule. It is evidence, not an action.

what to look at

Four things the fan makes visible

1 · Coral moves down the column. In none it is the first button; in handoff it is still the first button but the second one is gone entirely. The eye lands in the same place every time — that is the point of the attention law.
2 · The dot appears twice, never three times. Only 지금 찾기 and 인계하기 get it, because only those two are backed by something live (runners online / a runner standing at the gate).
3 · Below 동네 nothing changes between states. Browsing is state-independent, so the bottom two-thirds are identical in all three columns — which is why the top must carry all the signal.
4 · Confirmed is the long one. It carries 티켓 보기 + 채팅 + 미리 예약, and it is worth asking whether 채팅 earns a full row there or belongs inside the ticket screen. Open question, yours.
