왜 이 세 장인가

Grounds, one justification each

App frames are pure #FFFFFF — ④ and every sheet obey that without exception. The card is an export, not an app screen: it lands on somebody else's feed, so it is allowed its own ground, and each one is a decision:

① photo + white apron. The photo is content (the shipped 사진법: no stock, no wallpaper), the apron is the same white paper as the app, so the card and the screen it came from are visibly one product.

② pure white, no photo. A shared white card reads as a certificate and survives any feed background behind it. It is also the honest state: 0 photos is common (the runner is nudged, never forced — runner/done.tsx), and this card needs none.

③ photo full-bleed + one ink plate (#26231b). Story format is edge-to-edge by convention. White type cannot be measured against an unknown photo, so the numbers sit on an ink plate — the repo's own idiom — at about 14:1. No white on coral anywhere.

The stage is not a scrim

The card sits on --recess, the existing neutral. A share-sheet scrim would be a new dark value nobody ruled, and on device the OS draws its own anyway. What the lab owes him is the card and the doors, at true size.

🔴 v2 — NOT IN THIS LAB

Scoped out, not forgotten, and each needs its own consent surface before it can be drawn:

지도 · 실측 경로 · 코스 실루엣. A trace is location data about where a dog lives — that is the whole reason 0060 / 0065 / 0122 / 0123 exist. A blurred or "stylised" trace is still the shape of the walk home, so v1 draws none at all.

크루 · 다른 아이 이름 · 다른 아이 사진. Other people's data. The feed path already refuses a run containing a non-consenting dog (photo_consent, club/receipt/[bid].tsx) — the export path has no equivalent gate, so v1 simply carries one dog.

장소 문자열 (잠수교, 집결지, 주소). A place is a location datum even as five Korean characters. 반포 하이클럽 is a club, not a place.

러너 이름 — a real person who never chose to be published. 돈 — 하이 포인트, 정산, any amount: a card is a record, not a receipt.

⚠ Cost of the scope, stated plainly: 초코 and 잠수교 5km are in the other five labs' fixture set and are missing here on purpose. That is the scope working, not the fixtures drifting.

What already ships, and what changes

The route is not new: /shot/[bid] is a five-skin 인증샷 스튜디오 and both owner/report.tsx and club/receipt/[bid].tsx already open it. Two things are new. Its skins are in the retired volt/cream palette, predating every round-3/4/5 rule; and run-share-card.tsx draws the GPS trace onto the exported image. The most public surface in the product currently exports a course line — that is the line this lab removes.

The nudge is an offer, not a trap

One coral door (카드 만들기 → /shot/[bid], a route that exists), one quiet 나중에, and a sentence saying what the card leaves out before he taps rather than after. Nothing auto-posts: 공유하기 hands the PNG to the OS sheet, 인스타 스토리로 opens Instagram's own composer, and the app never publishes anything by itself.

No dead buttons in any state: the Instagram row is absent — not greyed — when instagramAvailable() is false (drawn in ②), and a run with no photos still has a card, because that card is ②.

Affordance count

Tappable per frame: ① back-free sheet = 3 · ② = 2 · ③ = 3 · ④ back + 카드 만들기 + 나중에 = 3. The card itself is an image and carries zero tap marks — the numerals, the club line and the mark are print.
