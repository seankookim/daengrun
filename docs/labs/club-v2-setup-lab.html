현장 인계 is drawn as the recommended default: the runner does no pickup leg, brings only themselves and their equipment, and learns zero addresses. 집 픽업 is neither disabled nor hidden — one tap away, fully drawn.

⚠ Two things this is NOT. It is not a model change: spec v2 §7.2 makes 집 픽업 the flag's default, and if he keeps the recommendation the copy leads with 현장 while the stored default stays whatever the spec session sets. And it prints no price — 현장 픽업 is very likely the cheaper option, but what a runner earns when the pickup leg disappears is open and his, so an amount or a 「더 저렴」 line would answer his own question back at him inside the artifact he uses to decide.

The choice row, re-drawn (R4)

ⓐ 눌린 키raised block + hard ink offset; the pick presses into its own shadow and inverts. Loudest, most tactile.
ⓑ 도장 칸no box at all — ink rules and an empty square that asks to be stamped. Quietest, most paper-native.
ⓒ 플레이트the binary as two half-width plates; the shape says "one of two" before a word is read.

All three survive colour removal: fill inversion, a stamped mark, position and weight carry the state. None is a bordered rectangle abutting another bordered rectangle — that shape is what read as a spreadsheet.

Not tappable, so unmarked

③'s seven detail rows, the 준비물 chips, the 인계 lines and the serials carry no fill, no chevron, no border box. They are print. The contrast is the information.

Every choice frame closes on the two-sided line. The shipped return is person-to-person, both-stamped — a screen that said "러너가 데려다 줍니다" and stopped would imply a one-sided handover the product does not do. ④'s line also names when the runner's responsibility ends, which is the doctrine the whole round-5 detour was about.
