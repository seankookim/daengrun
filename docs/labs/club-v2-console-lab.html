Pick by number — and three questions the pick answers

  1. Where does the evidence view live? ① and ③ keep it inline on the one scroll (always visible, always long); ② gives it a room of its own behind the 종료 tab (clean, but one tap away from the timeline it summarizes).
  2. How loud is recovery? ① puts the 재배정 strip in the urgent stack at the top; ② files it under the phase where it belongs; ③ attaches it beneath the gate item it exists to unblock. All three label it 재배정 and window-bound it — the question is prominence, not existence.
  3. One gate or two? Only ③ draws the two-phase finish (confirm → close) as two visible gates. If ① or ② wins, Gate 2's blockers (returns, case ownership) still need a home after the confirm tap — likely the done-state screen the console already has.
