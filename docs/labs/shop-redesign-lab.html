Honesty rules for this screen — what may NEVER render

  1. No discount, no strike-through, no 정가/할인가. We hold no price history for another retailer's SKU. A ₩41,000 harness renders as ₩41,000, full stop.
  2. No ratings, no ★, no review counts, no 판매량. daengrun has zero reviews for external products, and copying a marketplace's rating is republishing someone else's number as our own.
  3. No urgency. No 오늘만, no 마감 임박, no countdown, no 품절 임박 — there is no deal-window source and inventing one is the oldest dark pattern on this list.
  4. No stock counts. We do not hold inventory and cannot read theirs.
  5. No point-payment affordance — no 포인트로 결제, no 포인트 차감 preview, no 포인트 사용 toggle on any product. Brand-deals ⑤ is blocked behind take-rate. The balance may display; it may never look spendable here.
  6. No 최저가 / 무료배송 / 정품 보장 claims. Front Range sits at ₩41,000 on Coupang and ₩68,000 at PIGDOG — the research flags that spread as a parallel-import signal. We show the linked seller's price only, never a range and never a "lowest" claim.
  7. No fit claims. The app knows distance, pace, terrain and addons. It does not know paw condition, joint health, or size. Copy may say "이번 주 12.4km를 뛰었어요"; it may not say "초코 발에 무리가 왔어요" or "초코에게 맞는 사이즈예요".
  8. No weather line. There is no weather anywhere in this codebase — not in runs, not in bookings, not in any client fetch. The cooling-vest editorial line therefore binds month + hour from scheduled_at, never 기온/습도. See the bindings legend.
  9. No card without a live link. Before a deal is signed a card renders in its 준비 중 shape: no 제휴 chip, no price row, no 바로가기, not pressable. Signed deal or it doesn't render.
  10. No silent commerce. Leaving the app is announced — the outbound sheet names the seller and states that daengrun earns a commission, before the browser opens.
  11. No stale price without a date. Every price carries an 조회 기준일, and the whole screen carries one in the disclosure band.
  12. No dead affordance. Today's shop is six Alert() taps. If a control has no destination in a given state, it does not render in that state.

Each phone: the shelf in its affiliate-live state, then under the lab divider the day-0 pre-deal state that actually ships first, then the 하이 포인트 area in both its today shape and its ⑤-unlocked shape. Click ▶ 제휴 성사 to watch a card cross from pre-deal to live.

Chip grammar, decided once. 제휴 = we earn a commission if you buy through this link. 광고 = the brand paid for the slot itself. Both are outlined/filled ink marks, never coral — coral is the app's CTA and 라이브 colour, and a disclosure label that looks like a button is a disclosure that gets tapped by accident. Source pills (쿠팡 · 무신사 · 브랜드몰 · 삼성) are neutral — no brand colours are imitated, because a coloured 쿠팡 pill implies an endorsement relationship we do not have.

Why: the framing that scales down instead of up. Ⓔ① 태그 카드 — a price tag with a hole and a string

Why: the app's whole material world is printed paper — 여권, 탑승권, 부속서, 영수증, 도장. A price tag is the one retail object made of the same stuff, so the shelf joins the world instead of importing a marketplace grid into it. The perforated stub column is the same idiom already shipped in ClubBanner (84dp dashed stub), so this is a reuse, not a new grammar. Practically: the stub gives the source pill a permanent home, which is what stops the 쿠팡/무신사 label from floating around the card as the shelf grows.

Notes carried into implementation. (1) Nothing ships live. There is no affiliate_products table, no link store, no click event, and no approved program — Coupang's API is behind a ₩150,000 cumulative-sales gate and 무신사 큐레이터 is unapplied. Whatever Sean picks, v1 is the 준비 중 shape of that variant, and the live state lands one product at a time as deals sign. That is the "signed deal or it doesn't render" rule expressed as a build order, not a caveat. (2) The 제휴/광고 chip is a data field, not a style. It must come from the deal record (kind: 'affiliate' | 'ad'), so a card literally cannot render without one. A default of "no chip" is the failure mode 표시광고법 punishes. (3) Coupang's disclosure sentence is verbatim and mandated — "이 화면은 쿠팡 파트너스 활동의 일환으로, 이에 따른 일정액의 수수료를 제공받습니다". In an app there is no blog footer, so it lives on the outbound sheet (Ⓔ①'s play). Open question from the research §5.4: whether Coupang permits links inside a native app shelf at all is unverified — confirm before building the Coupang path. (4) Prices are snapshots. Every price needs an as-of date beside it and a refresh story behind it. A price stored once and rendered forever becomes a lie on its own schedule. (5) The editorial line is a fetch, and fetches fail. Every .ed block is independently optional and independently absent on error — a shop that half-renders its data lines must not render an empty violet box where a sentence should be. (6) 체력 나이 has a gate. fitnessGate returns {reason:'birth'} or {reason:'runs', left}; in both cases fitnessAge is null and RACK 01's line must not render. The gate reason is honest copy on 체력 리포트 — it is not a shop line. (7) The 하이 포인트 balance never touches a product. No per-card point affordance, no "이 상품 = 3,200P" equivalence, no basket. Balance lives in its own strip with 적립만 돼요 in its body. Brand-deals ⑤ unblocks with take-rate; the dashed row in Ⓢ② marks where it will attach. (8) Six dead Alert() taps are retired by this redesign: the 장바구니 circle, the 검색 field, the six-card grid's 준비 중 alerts and the + buttons. Anything without a destination does not render — the 부티크 미리보기 grid of six invented 도그스하이 에디션 SKUs goes with them, since none of those products exist. (9) 14pt floor, precedented exceptions only.
