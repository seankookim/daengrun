The logo drops into the phrase
and the phrase is the state

② taken all the way: the mark leaves the masthead and fills the ragged right of the display line, the reservation button is filled, and the big black phrase changes with every booking state. Seven states, the full scroll, and a hard pass on the club widget.

the move

Why dropping the logo works here

✓The display line is ragged by nature — 「오늘 3:30」 is short, 「초코가 달려요」 is long. That right-hand gap was dead space; the mark now occupies it, so the block reads as one composition instead of text with a hole.
✓The masthead stops competing. A centred logo above a 37pt phrase gave the eye two things to land on. Now there is one: the phrase, with the mark riding inside it.
✓It buys back the display-font budget — the wordmark no longer needs Black Han Sans at the top, so the phrase is the screen's single display use (DESIGN.md §3).
!The cost, and it bit immediately: the mark now lives inside copy that changes every state, so the phrase has to be written around it. The first draft of ③ read 「s4kim2025 러너의 / 응답을 기다려요」 and the name ran straight under the hound — a real collision, caught in the mock. Hence the law below.
the drop law
Line 1 of the phrase must end before the last ~100px. Names, places and numbers go on line 2 or in the sub.
Every state below obeys it. ⑤ handoff is the tightest legal case (mark drops to 36pt); ③ shows the rewrite that fixed the collision.

club widget · harsh pass

Ten treatments, each with what's wrong with it

The brief was premium. Below, each is judged against one test: does it look like a thing you own, or like a row in a list? ⑦ from the last lab is included as the baseline it has to beat.

①Engraved beige (baseline)
반포동 하이클럽
호스트 · 멤버 1명
Verdict: a plaque, not a thing you own. Centred and symmetric, so it reads as a sign on a wall. No serial, no date, nothing that says it is yours.
②Engraved + ledger foot
반포동 하이클럽
호스트 · 멤버 1명
EST. 2026 · 반포NO. —
Better. The foot rule turns a plaque into a document. The empty NO. is honest but visibly missing — it wants the server field.

The minimal that actually works. No box at all — a foil rule and a name, matching the screen's "선 0개, 상자 0개" grammar. Loses the object-ness of ③ but never fights the hero. The real alternative.
⑨Corner-fold card
반포동 하이클럽
호스트 · 멤버 1명
Decoration pretending to be structure. The fold means nothing — nothing is folded, nothing opens. Under the honesty laws a device that implies an affordance it does not have is the visual version of a dead button.

Closest to the record thesis. A ledger entry rather than a card — gold ink and a hairline do all the work. Weakest as an object, strongest as evidence. Pairs best with ⑧ if you want home to stay flat.
