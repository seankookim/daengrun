Three pairs — pick the one that should ship

  1. ①큰 시계 — a 38px ticking mm:ss is the screen's hero, with a depletion rule under it. Expiry then has to be generous, because the clock promised drama: it says the seat was let go and offers it straight back if room remains.
  2. ②줄 안의 시계 — the amount is the hero and the clock is one inline numeral in a record line. Expiry is the same register: a badge on the record, no ceremony.
  3. ③마감 시각 — no ticking at all. It prints the instant the hold ends (from hold_expires_at), which cannot drift or lie while the tab is backgrounded. Expiry names precisely what was lost and what was not.

Say a number — ①, ② or ③. Each column is two frames: the live hold on top, its own expiry screen below. Judge the pair, not the clock alone.

The three facts the copy is checked against

① Read the timestamp, not the label

_club_delegated_reserved (0043:69-78) stops counting a hold the instant hold_expires_at passes, while hold_status stays the string active until club_expire_delegation_holds() next runs. The seat and the label free at different instants. Every frame above is keyed on holdExpiresAt; a screen keyed on holdStatus would run a live countdown over a seat somebody else can already take.

② Expiry is not terminal

session_pay_delegation (0043:311-321) re-reads the hold under the session lock and, finding none, re-checks capacity and proceeds if there is room. The server's own push says the same sentence: 「자리 홀드가 풀렸어요 — 정원이 남아 있으면 다시 결제할 수 있어요」 (0043:392-394). So no ⓔ frame says 「끝났어요」 and none removes the pay route. When the room is genuinely gone the call raises no_capacity, and that is the refusal a person should meet — a failure shown as a failure, not a button hidden in advance.

🔴 ③ The corollary that is not built — a console question, not a silent decision

The ruling's closing line: 「when an OPEN-A hold expires unpaid, the signup releases and access ends with it」. Measured: it does not. _club_shell_access (0049:9-25) grades on approval = 'approved' and service_state <> 'ended' and never reads hold_status, so an expired-unpaid owner keeps exactly the grade they had. Nothing revokes it.

What ③ doesprints the three-line 그대로/풀림 ledger, so the screen states the true state instead of implying a revocation.
What is owedSean's own instruction — 「it comes back as a new console question」. The question is: does an expired hold end read access, or only the seat?

⚠ It is not a free choice either way. Ending access on expiry means a person who was reading the chat two minutes ago is thrown out of it while still approved; keeping access means 「signed-up gets access」 quietly outlives the seat it came with. That trade belongs to Sean, and no lab may settle it by drawing one arm.

The amount is derived, not invented

club_fare(km) = 9900 + round(km × 3000) (0043:14). A 5 km 잠수교 loop is 9,900 + 15,000 = 24,900원. Change the route and every frame's number moves with it.
