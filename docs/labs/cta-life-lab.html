Life without lying

The constraint that rules them all: the repo already names the failure mode — 지어낸 긴급함 = 학습된 무시 (invented urgency becomes learned ignoring). So the motion must read as invitation, never as alarm. Anything that pulses like a notification is disqualified no matter how good it looks.

what ships with it

Three rules attached to whichever you pick

1 · Only the primary moves. If the ink button also animated, the screen would have two invitations and the coral would stop meaning "press this".
2 · Motion stops when it is not your turn. In searching/directed/active the primary is ink and static — there is nothing to hurry toward. Coral moves only when the app is actually waiting on you.
3 · prefers-reduced-motion kills every loop, keeps the depth and the press. This page already obeys it — turn the setting on and the sheen and nudge stop while the button stays pressable.
