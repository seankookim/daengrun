B · The seven laws, and where to check them

LawHow it is satisfied above
1 · face ≥65%The lab measures itself: the block beside each phone is computed live in your browser from real bounding boxes, across every card, not a sample. Verified in Chromium at 2× — ① 67.5% · ② 67.1% · ③ 67.6% · ④ 68.7–71.1%, floor 65%.
2 · one type jumpName → price is the only hierarchy. Exactly two sizes below 15pt per card (11.5 latin kicker, 14 body/chip) — failure-checklist #6 stays clear.
3 · hairline chromeOne radius (14), one hairline colour, one ground per direction. No racks, gates, tickets, rails, tape or perforations anywhere. --coral appears nowhere inside a phone.
4 · fixed fieldsEvery card in a direction carries the identical field set in the identical order, including the state pill. Missing data is stated (스트랩 별도), never dropped.
5 · editorial interstitialOne story panel per shelf, sitting between blocks. Nothing is overlaid on a face; no editorial data line under any card.
6 · purpose nav첫 러닝 · 여름 · 발바닥 · 야간 안전. Module titles name their rule and count (네 가지 / 두 가지 / 다섯), never a mood.
7 · owned proofCredential row, claim-style descriptors, named curator byline, published firewall. No ratings, no review counts, no third-party rankings quoted as proof.

C · Honesty ledger

  • Absent by design: star ratings, review counts, stock counters, discount percentages, struck-through prices, countdowns, urgency copy, point-payment affordances, 적립 badges, fabricated context data (weather / paw-stress numbers inside a card).
  • Pre-deal state is one quiet pill reading 입고 준비 중 with the price still shown at full strength — SNKRS shows the shoe and changes the state, it does not dim the product or clutter the card with 준비 중 labels. Seven of ten items are pre-deal today and the shelf is not embarrassed by it.
  • Disclosure runs three times and only three times: top of shelf (loud), per card (quiet, in the text area), outbound sheet (verbatim). Nothing is sprinkled.
  • Prices are the researched figures with their source recorded in affiliate-product-research.md; where two sources disagree (Front Range ₩41,000 Coupang / ₩68,000 PIGDOG) the shelf shows the price on the path we can actually link.

D · Judgment calls — argue with these

  • The study's card arithmetic is off by one line, and I corrected it rather than cheat the law. §(b) budgets an 84pt text stack under a 216pt well for a 300pt card (72%). That only holds if the product name is one line. Holding the study's own LAW 4 — a fixed two-line name slot on every card — the stack measures ~125pt, which would have put the well at 60.6% and failed LAW 1. So the well grew instead of the text shrinking: ① 173×268, ② 195×272, ③ 358×448 (the study's 4:5, unchanged), ④ 390×470. The law was the thing to keep; the 216 was an estimate.
  • Brand hexes are placeholders. The study sanctions three by name — Ruffwear ochre, HOWLPOT lilac, Aesop's amber-brown ground. LILA LOVES IT sage and Samsung graphite are my approximations. Every field must be sampled from the brand's own site before this ships; a colour is a fact, and these are not yet sourced facts.
  • Ⓥ③/Ⓥ④ run five text lines under the face, which trips failure-checklist #4 ("more than three text lines").
