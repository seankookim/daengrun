Honesty notes carried into implementation. (1) Every count must filter runs.end_reason='completed' — bookings.status='completed' alone also covers early-terminated settlements, so a 조기 종료 run would silently inflate 완주 stamps. (2) 연속 2주 is the historical maximum week streak, never the live one — a stamp that can be lost is not a stamp. (3) 응가 도장 counts poop_bonus ledger rows, which settle_run_tx writes once per booking; the copy therefore says 러닝 N회, never 응가 N번. (4) Stamp rotation angles are a fixed per-key table, not Math.random() — a stamp's tilt is part of its identity and must survive re-render. (5) Loading is not zero: until the derivations resolve, the wall renders its slot outlines with no counts rather than "0 / 12". (6) Stamps are inert in v1 — there is no stamp-detail screen, and a tappable element with no destination is a dead button. (7) 14pt floor, one precedented exception.
