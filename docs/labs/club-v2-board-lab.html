Three genuinely different dashboard personalities below, all binding the same NEW sanitized projection club_session_board and all visibly obeying the privacy rows: no addresses ever, no money ever, a pick in progress reads 수락 대기 with no runner name to third parties (pairs are public, courtships are not), dog first-name + photo and owner display name are the allowed identity set. Every frame is 390px so 1px = 1pt; zero hexes outside theme.ts / club-ui.tsx.

Privacy laws every frame must visibly respect (§9, verified precedents)

① no address, no area band, on any board surface (§8.4) · ② no fare, no fee, no payout — money speaks only on the 승낙서 (ruling ④) · ③ 수락 대기 renders WITHOUT the courted runner's name; the pairing becomes public only at P4 (0053:315-323 sub-gate inverted) · ④ a 맹견-refused or lapsed pick is indistinguishable from a declined one · ⑤ crew rows carry name + role only; roster/phone data never joins the board (0049).
