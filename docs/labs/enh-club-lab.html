⚠ Cluster rule — club exits are not the marketplace ladder

Club bookings are excluded from the 0066 marketplace cancel ladder and the schedule sheet already routes them away (owner/schedule.tsx:663 — 위탁 예약은 클럽 세션 화면에서 처리해요). The club has its own exits, in its own words, judged by its own server function (0048 session_cancel_delegation → club_cfg('cancel_free_hours'|'cancel_late_pct'|'cancel_post_accept_pct')). Nothing in this lab may quote a marketplace fee, a 50% 러너 보상 arm, or 「러너 변경」 — those words do not exist in this world.

What the code already gets right — and none of it is up for cutting

  • Three states, three sentences. LoadGate separates 불러오는 중 / 불러오지 못했어요 / 권한 없음 on every deep-linked club screen, and 돌아가기 is always mounted. No variant here removes a state.
  • Price invisibility (재정 ④). The club fare is spoken once, on the 승낙서, and nowhere else. The session shell deliberately holds board.session.fare and never draws it. Two variants below get close to that line; both stop at the percentage and hand the won amount to Sean as a question.
  • Dead-button refusals are already named. The console's runner chips print 만석 / 체크인 전 / 배정 창 닫힘 rather than failing after the tap. K② extends that grammar; it does not invent it.
  • Loud failures. 트레이스 저장이 밀리고 있어요, 비상 연락처를 불러오지 못했어요 — 다시 시도, 견적을 불러오지 못했어요. These are content (§7b), and they survive every variant.
