# Naver Cafe Presence Plan — the legitimate version

*Drafted 2026-07-30. Replaces the "agent-run persona accounts" idea. Principle (locked): **no undisclosed commercial identity, ever.** 뒷광고 is 표시광고법 exposure, Naver ToS violation, and — worse for us — the single fastest way to die in the exact channel we need. We are a trust product; we don't manufacture trust. What agents CAN do is everything behind the keyboard except pretend to be someone.*

---

## 0. The three lanes

| Lane | Account | Discloses? | Who/what writes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Founder persona | Real founder account, real name | Yes — "도그스하이 만드는 사람" in every relevant post | Sean posts; agents draft/research |
| 2. Founding-member reviews | Members' own accounts | Organic — genuinely uncompensated, so nothing to disclose; if any perk is attached, member discloses it | Members write; we only ask |
| 3. Agent back office | No public account | n/a | Agents: listening, drafting, alerting |

## 1. Lane 1 — the founder persona (창업일지 content)

Korean cafe/커뮤니티 culture rewards transparent builders. A founder openly documenting "한강에서 개랑 뛰는 서비스를 만들고 있습니다" gets warmth that no brand account can buy — and it inoculates: when the service launches, the community already knows the person behind it.

- **Where**: 2–3 targets max. One large 반려견 카페 (강사모-type), one launch-동 지역맘/동네 카페, plus 당근 동네생활 (same rules apply there). Read each cafe's 홍보 규정 first — many require 등업 + activity history and ban promo outright except in designated boards. Follow them to the letter; get the 카페지기's blessing before anything that smells commercial.
- **Cadence**: 1 post/week + genuine comment activity 3–4x/week. Months, not weeks — this lane compounds slowly and that's fine.
- **Content mix (80/20 rule — 80% pure value, 20% journey)**:
  - Dog exercise science: 견종별 적정 운동량, 슬개골 안전, 여름 발바닥 화상/heat-index 가이드 (our runner cert curriculum, given away — the curriculum IS the trust asset)
  - Launch-동 specific: best 한강 진입로 with dogs, 새벽/야간 러닝 코스 리뷰
  - Build-log: 러너 인증 시험에서 몇 명 떨어졌는지, 바디캠 왜 달았는지 — honest, specific, numbers included
  - Never: covert product pitches inside "info" posts. When it's about the service, say so in the first line.
- **The ask ratio**: at most 1 in 10 posts mentions anything bookable, and only in boards where the cafe allows it.

## 2. Lane 2 — founding-member reviews (the real trust engine)

Playbook already defines it; operationalize it:

- The ask happens at the **photo-delivery moment** (event finisher photo or first-run recap card — peak delight): "사진 마음에 드시면, 카페에 솔직한 후기 한 줄 부탁드려도 될까요? 좋았던 것만 쓰지 마시고요." The explicitly-invited-criticism line matters — it produces reviews that read real because they are.
- **No compensation for reviews.** Free founding runs are for being a founding member, never contingent on posting. If we ever run a perk-attached campaign, the member's post carries 대가 표시 per 표시광고법 — but the default program is simpler: no perk, no strings, so there's nothing to disclose and nothing to be caught for.
- Target: 2–3 genuine member posts/month in local cafes during Phase 1–2. That's enough — in a 동-sized community, 10 real reviews over a quarter is saturation.
- Screenshot assets travel with the review: GPS trail, 체력나이 card, finisher photo. Real artifacts, member-owned.

## 3. Lane 3 — the agent back office (where your automation idea lives)

Everything you wanted agents to do, minus the fake identity:

1. **Brand + demand listening**: scheduled agent sweeps of target cafes, 당근, X, Instagram for (a) mentions of 도그스하이 (b) under-exercised-dog complaint threads ("산책으로 부족", "에너지 감당 안 됨", 견종별 문제행동) (c) competitor mentions (비포펫/와요 experiences — their complaints are our positioning research). Output: a weekly digest with links + suggested response drafts. (This is a /brand-listening run on a schedule — we can set it up whenever you want.)
2. **Draft, don't post**: agents draft founder-persona posts and reply comments from the weekly digest; Sean reviews, edits into his own voice, posts from his own account. Rule: nothing publishes without a human hand on it, and everything publishes from a disclosed identity.
3. **Reputation alarm**: any negative mention or incident thread triggers same-day alert. In cafe culture the response window is hours — a sincere founder reply in-thread on day 0 typically defuses; silence for a week becomes lore.
4. **Compliance guard**: agent reviews planned posts against each cafe's 홍보 규정 and flags anything that reads as undisclosed promo before it goes out.

## 4. Why this beats the sockpuppet version even on pure ROI

- Fake-account farming needs months of aging anyway — same timeline as the founder persona, with a fatal downside attached and zero compounding asset at the end.
- 맘카페 forensics (account age, posting pattern, 등업 history, writing style) catch operators routinely; the exposé thread then owns your brand's search results. For a GPS+bodycam trust product, that's not a marketing setback, it's positioning suicide.
- The founder persona converts *better* per post: "만드는 사람이 직접 답해줌" is a differentiator no competitor's marketing agency can fake back at us.

## 5. 90-day cadence

Weeks 1–2: pick the 2–3 cafes, 등업 grind, read 규정, first two value posts (heat-safety guide is the summer opener). Weeks 3–6: weekly value posts + first member reviews land post-event; listening digest running. Weeks 7–12: build-log momentum ("러너 1기 인증 결과"), 카페지기 relationship → ask about an official 공구/이벤트 slot (many cafes sell sanctioned promo slots — that's the legitimate paid placement, use it instead of stealth).

Metric: branded search volume + cafe-sourced founding-member signups (ask "어디서 보셨어요?" at signup — one field, gold data).
