# Runner Recruitment Campaign — DOGS HIGH

*Drafted 2026-07-30. Companion to docs/marketing-fundraising.md (§2 runner dialect, §3 Phase 0–1) and docs/growth-model (Supply sheet). Runner dialect throughout: Nike-coded, athletic identity — never "알바 구함."*

## 0. Strategy in one line

We don't hire dog walkers. We certify runners. The pitch is a **credential with income attached**, not a job — because the supply we need (pace-tested, dog-safe, reliable) only comes from people who'd run anyway, and because scarcity is what makes owners trust the marketplace.

Target: **20–30 certified runners in the launch 동 before demand launch** (Supply model: 50 paying dogs need ~15 utilized / ~22 certified runners at 1.5x oversupply).

## 1. The honest earnings math (publish the math, never the claim)

2026 minimum wage: **₩10,320/hr**. Our per-run payout at base assumptions (5km avg, 33% take): **~₩16,700/run**.

| Scenario | Gross to runner | Time incl. handover | Effective ₩/hr |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single 5km run | ₩16,683 | ~60 min | ~₩16,700 (1.6x min wage) |
| 7km run | ₩20,703 | ~70 min | ~₩17,700 (1.7x) |
| 2 back-to-back 5km (same meet point) | ₩33,366 | ~110 min | ~₩18,200 (1.8x) |
| Club session (2-dog cap, vet/master tier) | 2 bookings | ~70 min | highest ₩/hr in system |

Rules for ads and info sessions:

1. **Show the table, not a slogan.** "시급 2만원!" invites 과장광고 exposure and churns runners when a slow week disappoints. "회당 1.6만원대 — 시간당으로 직접 계산해보세요" recruits the ones who'll stay.
2. Never promise volume. Say "수요는 동네 밀도에 따라 다릅니다 — 초기 러너일수록 배정 우선."
3. The comparison that lands with our audience isn't minimum wage anyway — it's **"어차피 뛸 러닝에 페이가 붙는다"**. Zero marginal time cost is the real pitch.
4. Take rate **decided 2026-08-05: 33% flat** (migration 0059) — this table is computed at 33%. It remains a live lever (Assumptions!B13); if it ever changes again, recompute this table *before* printing anything.

## 2. Two audiences, one funnel

### A. Running crews (primary — quality first)

Who: 20–30s crew members in the launch 동, Strava users, 러닝크루 culture. They bring pace, reliability, and *content* (they already post their runs).

Mechanics:

- Partner with 2–3 crews in the launch 동. Offer: post-crew-run coffee + 10-min info session, crew leader gets first cert slot + ambassador fee per certified member (₩20–30k, capped).
- **Certification as flex**: physical patch + bib + profile badge + Strava club membership. "기준을 통과한 러너만 배정합니다" — mirror 와요's top-10% acceptance flex.
- DOGS HIGH Strava club from day one: certified runners only. The club feed *is* the recruitment ad — every logged dog-run with volt bib photos recruits the next runner for free.
- Copy anchors (existing brand lines): **"뛰던 길에서, 벌자."** / **"당신의 러닝에 심박수 하나 더."** / login tagline mirror: "러너스 하이를, 우리 아이에게" flips to "너의 러너스 하이에, 한 명 더."

### B. University students (volume + schedule flexibility)

Who: 대학생/휴학생 wanting income on their own schedule — break-time and term-time both. The "no boss, no shift, no minimum hours" structure is genuinely differentiated vs 카페 알바; say exactly that and nothing more.

Mechanics:

- Recruit narrow, not broad: campus running clubs and 체대/스포츠과학과 first — they pass the pace test and handle dogs confidently. General 에브리타임 blasts produce applicants who fail cert and waste onboarding time.
- Channels: 캠퍼스픽 + 링커리어 (대외활동/서포터즈 boards — frame as "1기 러너 모집," certification cohort, not 알바 posting), campus crew Instagram accounts, 체대 학생회 단톡 via one warm intro, university 러닝 소모임 in the launch 동's nearby campuses.
- Framing: **"1기 인증 러너"** — first-generation cohort identity, numbered certificates. Gen Z responds to founding-member scarcity; "알바 모집" gets scrolled past, "1기 30명" gets screenshotted.
- Schedule pitch: "시험기간엔 안 뛰면 됨. 방학엔 매일 뛰면 됨. 승인도 상사도 없음." (all true — keep it true.)

### The funnel (both audiences)

```
See post/crew session → info session RSVP (form) → pace test + dog-handling onboarding
→ 신원인증 + insurance enrollment → CERTIFIED (badge, bib, patch, Strava club)
→ first run within 7 days (critical — assign manually if needed) → active
```

Instrument every step. Expected drop-offs are the product: a hard cert bar is the brand. Report "X% 통과" publicly — rejection rate is marketing.

## 3. Creative concepts (runner dialect)

1. **"출근 말고 출주" 15s reel** — alarm at 6am, shoes on, dog waiting at the meet point, sunrise 한강, payout notification at the end. Fast cuts, breathing, paws + shoes in sync.
2. **Cert-day content** — every certification cohort gets a "합격" photo set (bib, number, dog). Runners post it themselves; it reads as an achievement, not employment.
3. **Earnings-math carousel** — the table from §1 as a clean IG carousel. Honesty as aesthetic: "계산해봐. 우리가 먼저 보여줄게."
4. **Crew takeover** — one partnered crew's Saturday run co-branded once a month; their content, our bibs.

## 4. Budget & targets (first 6 weeks)

- Info sessions x4 (coffee): ₩200k · Ambassador fees: ₩300k · Bibs/patches for 30: ₩400k · Boosted IG for recruitment posts: ₩300k → **~₩1.2M total.**
- Targets: 80+ info-session RSVPs → 50 pace-test takers → **25–30 certified** → 20+ complete first run within 7 days. Track cert-pass rate and week-1 activation as the two health metrics.
- Supply health metric ongoing: runs/runner/week ≥4 median (capacity is 8; below 3 = churn risk — feed them club sessions).

## 5. What we don't do

No 알바몬/알바천국 (wrong frame, wrong applicants). No earnings claims we can't screenshot. No mass campus posters before the 동's demand exists — runners who certify and get zero bookings in month 1 churn *and* badmouth. Supply follows the density plan, not the other way around.
