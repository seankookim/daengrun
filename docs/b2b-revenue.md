# B2B Revenue & Self-PR — revenue outside consumer bookings

*Drafted 2026-08-01. Companion to docs/marketing-fundraising.md, docs/growth-model.xlsx, docs/hangang-event-playbook.md. Honest framing up front: pre-PMF, B2B is (a) cost-offset, (b) credibility, (c) a pipeline you warm now and harvest at density. It does not replace consumer revenue in year one — the growth model's take-rate problem gets solved by take rate and retention, not by sponsor deals. Cap founder time on B2B at ~10% until 50 paying dogs.*

---

## 0. The asset we're actually selling

Not reach — **audience quality**. "Verified, high-spend, high-engagement dog owners, physically assembled weekly, with per-dog activity data" is a segment no Korean pet brand can buy anywhere else. Every B2B product below is a different wrapper around that one asset. Corollary: the asset doesn't exist until the Saturday event runs and the app logs runs — which sets the outreach timing (§4).

## 1. The revenue ladder (near → far)

### R1. Event sponsorship — now (cost-offset)
Snack-table / booth sponsorship per hangang-event-playbook §2. ₩200k + product per slot, 4 slots/mo, sell 2. Year-one job: make events free and start brand relationships. Not real revenue; the top of the funnel for everything below.

### R2. Sampling-as-a-service — from ~event 4 (first real invoices)
Brands pay for **targeted trial distribution**: goodie bag insert or booth sampling to N verified dog owners, with breed/size targeting ("소형견 가정 30곳") and a follow-up photo report. DTC pet brands burn real budget on trial acquisition; ₩5–10k per verified household delivered is defensible and honest. Package: sampling + finisher-photo logo + reel tag = ₩300–500k/event. Grows linearly with attendance; no product work.

### R3. Affiliate shop tab — light commerce before real commerce
The shop-tab commerce idea, de-risked: **curation + affiliate links, zero inventory**. 쿠팡 파트너스 as the default rail (harnesses, long lines, cooling vests, paw balm — running-specific gear) plus direct affiliate/promo-code deals with 2–3 brands from R1/R2. The differentiator is *contextual* recommendation from real activity: "이번 달 42km 뛴 아이 → 하네스 점검 시기" converts like advice, not ads.
- Rules: label every affiliate link (표시광고법 — same disclosure discipline as the cafe plan), recommend by fit not commission (정직 원칙 — the trust brand IS the margin), keep it inside the existing 샵 셸 v1 without new backend.
- Realistic scale: pocket money at 100 dogs, meaningful at 1,000. Build the habit surface early, monetize the density later.

### R4. Insurance referral — the sleeping giant (quarter 2+)
Only **12.8% of Korean owners have pet insurance** (KB 2025); insurers pay real acquisition bounties, and our owners are pre-qualified (they pay for dog fitness). Start as a simple referral partnership (삼성화재·메리츠·DB 등 펫보험 라인 — pick one, exclusive, better terms).
- ⚠ Regulatory: actively *soliciting* insurance requires 보험대리점/모집종사자 registration — structure v1 as pure introduction/lead-gen and have the partner's counsel bless the flow before launch. Long-term angle worth planting in the first meeting: GPS-verified exercise data as an underwriting/wellness-discount signal ("활동견 할인") — that's a data partnership no walking app can offer.

### R5. Apartment 단지 contracts — B2B2C density play (post-PMF in launch 동)
입주자대표회의/관리사무소 buys a weekly on-site session or resident discount as a 커뮤니티 서비스 (premium 단지 compete on amenities). One contract = 10–30 dogs at once, CAC ≈ 0, and it's the direct counter to 커넥팅더닷츠's apartment-embedded strategy. Sell after the Saturday event is visibly working in that 동 — the event *is* the demo. Price as flat monthly (₩500k–1M/단지) + resident conversion upside.

### R6. Corporate pet benefits — later, opportunistic
Companies adding 펫복지 buy run-credit bundles as employee perks. Warm channel: any sponsor from R1/R2 whose HR you meet anyway, plus pet-industry employers themselves. Don't build anything for it — sell credits, redeem in-app, invoice manually until it repeats.

### R7. Data products — parked until scale + consent framework
Anonymized breed-level fitness benchmarks (pace, recovery, volume by breed/age) for food/insurance R&D. Genuinely unique data, genuinely premature. Requires explicit owner consent design; revisit at Series A. Never sell individual-level data — state this publicly early, it's a trust asset.

### Not doing: pay-for-recommendation hospitals
Clinics paying to be "recommended" poisons the trust product (and invites kickback optics). The vet relationship stays trust-infrastructure: booth staffing, co-signed exertion protocols, clearly-labeled local placement at most. If a hospital directory ever ships, selection by published criteria, never by payment.

## 2. Brand outreach mechanics

**Target list (build now, ~20 names in 4 tiers):** treats/food (템테이션급 대기업 라인보다 DTC — 바잇미, 페스룸, 하울팟 등 IG-native 브랜드가 의사결정 빠름), gear (하네스/러닝 용품), insurance (펫보험 3사 중 1), platforms (핏펫·페오펫 — 커머스 제휴 + 투자사 SparkLabs 연결고리 겸용). Names above are candidates to verify, not commitments — 담당자 발굴은 IG DM + 회사 홈페이지 제휴 메일로 충분한 단계.

**Sequence per brand (low-pressure ladder):**
1. Warm open — event invite, no ask: "토요일 한강에서 이런 걸 합니다, 구경 오세요" + 3 best photos.
2. First paid yes — one sampling slot (₩200–300k). Small enough to expense without a meeting.
3. Report back — same-week photo report + reel tag. Over-deliver here; this report is the retention product.
4. Package — quarterly slot bundle or affiliate/promo-code deal.

**Rules:** sell real numbers only (attendance from actual events — no projected-reach media kits; the 정직 원칙 applies to B2B exactly as to consumers). One-pager 제안서, not a deck. Track every contact in a simple sheet (brand, tier, contact, stage, next action) from day one — 20 relationships is memory-manageable, 60 isn't.

## 3. Self-PR (earned media — free, compounds into fundraising AND B2B)

**The angles, ranked by pitchability:**
1. **Category creation** — "국내 최초 반려견 러닝 마켓플레이스" / "산책 말고 러닝". Startup media loves a category-first claim with a Wag-vs-Rover economics take attached.
2. **The gig-economy positive story** — 대학생·러닝크루가 어차피 뛰는 러닝으로 최저임금 2배를 버는 구조. Rare *good-news* labor angle; pitch with the honest earnings table from runner-recruitment.md.
3. **The visual** — dog bibs + Han River leaderboard ceremony. Photo-led pitches to lifestyle/pet media; GQ Korea ran canicross editorial (2025.9) — pitch them the "그 다음 이야기: 마켓플레이스가 됐다" follow-up.
4. **Founder story** — UChicago 재학생이 서울에서 창업. Use for fundraising-adjacent press, not the lead angle.

**Targets:** 스타트업 — 플래텀, 벤처스퀘어, 아웃스탠딩, wowtale (K-스타트업 본선 진출 시 자동 보도 자산). 펫 업계 — 데일리벳, 노트펫. 라이프스타일 — GQ/에스콰이어류 + 러닝 전문 매체/크루 계정.

**Timing rule:** pitch only after there are 2–3 events' worth of real photos and one round number ("매주 토요일, 누적 N마리"). Press wants the picture; the leaderboard ceremony shot is the whole pitch. One good article does triple duty: investor deck credibility slide, brand-outreach trust signal, cafe-plan Lane-1 material ("기사 나왔어요" is a legitimate founder-persona post).

**Prep (one afternoon, do in August):** boilerplate 3문장 + founder bio + 5 approved photos + fact sheet (모두 실측치) in docs/press-kit/. Reuse verbatim for K-스타트업 application, sponsor one-pager, and every pitch.
