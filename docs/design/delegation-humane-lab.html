RULESPhotography law — where warmth lives, where paper rules

5 slots, no moreClub cover (the one big photo) · live polaroid · receipt print · dog's book · group shot card. A sixth photo surface needs a reason this list doesn't cover.
Paper zonesPayment, consent, console, roster, case/incident, cancel: photo-free forever. Money and safety read on paper, warmth lives next door.
Scrim lawText on photos only within scrim zones (ink fade top, lilac fade bottom). No raw-photo text, no exceptions — legibility is non-negotiable.
Self-sourced onlyRunner shares, group shots, host covers. No stock photography anywhere, ever — a fake morning would poison the real ones.
Consent gateGroup shots and any photo showing people/dogs other than your own ride the existing photoConsent flag from the delegation consent — unconsented faces stay out of others' books.
Credit lineEvery displayed photo carries ⓒ + when + who, in mono microtype. Humane means someone made it.

The argument in one line: paper where money and safety live, photographs where the morning lives — warmth as content, not decoration.
