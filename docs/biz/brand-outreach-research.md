# Daengrun (도그스하이) — Korean Pet Brand Outreach Research

**Researched:** 2026-08-05 · All facts below were web-verified during this session.
**Tagging convention:** `[verified]` = read directly off a live page or article this session. `[likely]` = reasonable inference from verified facts, explicitly labelled as inference.
**Contact rule:** every email/phone/handle listed was found on a live page. Nothing here is invented. Where a channel is a general/CS or data-protection address rather than a stated partnership inbox, it is flagged as such.

**B2B ladder recap:** R1 = brand sponsors a Saturday club run (snacks/sampling on site, event fee). R2 = paid sampling program (brand's product handed to a verified dog-owner household mid-run, photo proof + follow-up report). R3 = affiliate / promo-code deal.

---

## Executive Summary

**The four previously-named candidates: 2 of 4 verified, 2 could not be verified.**

- 바잇미 (Bite Me) — **verified, and the single strongest target found.** It is the only Korean pet brand located this session that already runs *all three* rungs of the ladder in-house: a recurring 체험단 sampling program, a commission-based creator/affiliate program, and a dedicated B2B wholesale storefront. HQ is in Gangnam-gu.
- 페스룸 (PETHROOM) — **verified, and it has a real, public partnership intake board** with an explicit "기타 (other business type)" category, which is the door for a service partner like daengrun.
- 페티즌 — **could not verify.** No Korean pet brand under this name surfaced across multiple searches. The only near-match is "Petizen Limited" on LinkedIn, which does not present as a Korean pet brand. Treat as a dead lead unless Sean has a source.
- 댕러민 — **could not verify.** No brand, product, or company under this name surfaced. Searches returned only generic 댕댕이 slang results. Treat as a dead lead.

**The three biggest surprises, in order of strategic weight:**

1. **Seoul city already runs a free weekly running crew out of 반포한강공원 — daengrun's exact pilot location — and it is live right now.** `7979 서울 러닝크루` operates 2026-04-16 through 2026-10-29, Thursdays 19:00–21:00, from three hubs, one of which is Banpo Riverside Park with four courses from 2.8K to 8.2K. In its 2023 season it ran a dedicated 반려런 (dog run) session and was gear-sponsored by Descente. This is simultaneously the strongest proof that the sponsorship model works in this exact geography, a ready-made audience, and a potential co-host.
2. **A direct competitor already sells the R2 product.** 펫피 (PetP), a points-based dog walking app, sells 꼼꼼체험단 sampling, in-app ads, and custom marketing projects to brands, and has a B2B case-study section plus a downloadable partnership brochure. Daengrun's differentiation is not "sampling via a walking app" — that exists. It is **verified handler + mid-activity delivery + photo proof**, versus PetP's self-serve at-home model.
3. **아르르 (ARRR) is Dongwon F&B's pet brand and its mail-order registration is filed in Seocho** — the same district as Banpo. It launched 1,000-won-tier treats through Daiso in July 2026, meaning it has both corporate sponsorship budget and a sub-1,000-won unit cost that makes R2 sampling economics trivially easy.

**Recommended opening move:** lead with 바잇미 for R2+R3 (they have the machinery already, so there's nothing to educate them on), and 아르르 for R1 (corporate budget, local, cheap units). Do not open with insurance — it's a later-stage rung.
