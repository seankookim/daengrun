## 0. TL;DR

- **The category is empty.** Nobody in Korea — or as a marketplace anywhere in the world — sells dog *running*. Walking marketplaces exist (와요, 비포펫, 도그메이트) and one (Wag, US) went bankrupt doing exactly what we must avoid: low-ticket on-demand walks. 댕런 wins by creating a new premium category ("산책 말고 러닝"), selling recurring memberships, not single walks.
- **Marketing = density, not reach.** No bus ads, no TV, no pamphlets in year one. Pick one Han-River-adjacent 동, saturate it via 러닝크루 recruitment, 멍스타그램 seeding, and a weekly branded Han River dog-run event. The app's own trust stack (bodycam/GPS/photos) is the content engine.
- **Two brand dialects.** Owner-facing: trust + guilt-relief + dog joy. Runner-facing: Nike-coded athletic identity ("뛰던 길에서 벌자"). Same brand, opposite energy.
- **Fundraising = equity-free first.** The 2026 정부지원 windows (예창패, 초창패, 청창사, 창업중심대학) are all closed — they reopen Jan–Mar 2027. **올해의 K-스타트업 2026 is open until Aug 31** — apply now. Critical constraint: 예비창업패키지 2027 requires NO 사업자등록 at announcement time → incorporation timing is a strategic decision, not paperwork.
- **⚠️ Naming risk:** "댕댕런/댕런" is already used by Animal Lace for dog-marathon events (댕댕이레이스). Trademark check (KIPRIS) needed before spending anything on brand.

### Dog running specifically

- **Korea: zero dedicated dog-running services found.** Only how-to content and canicross editorial (GQ Korea, Sep 2025).
- **US: running exists only as small local operator businesses** (Run My Pup, Denver Dog Joggers) — never as a marketplace category. Wag and Rover have no running SKU.
- **Wag's bankruptcy (Chapter 11 completed Sep 2025, ~$69M cumulative losses)** is the cautionary tale: on-demand, low-ticket, walking-led marketplaces bleed cash. Rover won with higher-ticket boarding/sitting and sold to Blackstone for $2.3B (2024). → 댕런 must be **subscription-led (주 2–3회 러닝 멤버십), high AOV, premium-priced** from day one.

### Positioning implications

1. **Never compete on walk price.** 비포펫 owns ₩9,900. 댕런 prices as premium fitness — closer to 유치원-per-outcome (₩25–40k/session) or above 와요's ₩19,500, justified by outcome (체력나이, exertion data), not minutes.
2. **Trust stack is table stakes, not differentiation.** GPS + reports + verification are standard; 도그메이트 did bodycams in the 2010s. Differentiate on *running-specific* trust nobody has: runner pace/fitness certification, breed/age exertion protocols (vet-informed), heat-index safety rules, post-run recovery report. This is also the TIPS "tech" story.
3. **Incoming threat: 커넥팅더닷츠** (childcare platform 째깍악어 + 도그메이트) is building childcare-grade-vetted, apartment-embedded pet care in Seoul from late 2025 — they will own the generic "trust" narrative with corporate backing. Speed + category ownership ("running, not care") is the defense.
4. **Demand signal for every deck and ad:** KB금융 2025 반려동물보고서 — **41.2% of owners name regular exercise as a top pet-wellness priority**; monthly pet spend ₩194,000 and rising; Seoul+Gyeonggi hold 51.7% of Korea's 5.91M pet households → density strategy is backed by data.
5. **Regulatory:** walking/running 대행 without facility custody appears outside 동물위탁관리업 registration scope (와요 structures as 통신판매중개자). Gray zone = future regulation risk; our insurance + verification stack is both hedge and marketing asset (only 12.8% of owners have pet insurance — bundle coverage into every booking and say so loudly).
6. **⚠️ Naming collision:** Animal Lace runs "댕댕런"-branded dog marathon events. Two-sided implication: (a) KIPRIS trademark search + filing ASAP, possibly rename; (b) their events are a perfectly-targeted user acquisition channel — sponsor/attend regardless of naming outcome.

## 2. Brand & creative concepts

### The two-dialect system

One brand, two emotional registers — because the two sides have opposite anxieties.

**Owner dialect — 신뢰 + 죄책감 해소 + 강아지의 기쁨.** The owner's real emotion is guilt ("오늘도 못 뛰어줬다") plus fear of strangers. Never intense, never loud. Warm, evidence-forward, dog-centric. Every asset shows the *proof layer*: GPS trail, bodycam frame, photo check-in, 체력나이 improving.

- Category-creation line: **"산책 말고, 러닝."**
- Guilt-relief line: **"오늘도 못 뛰어준 미안함, 댕런이 대신 뜁니다."**
- Trust line: **"모든 러너는 신원인증. 모든 러닝은 GPS와 카메라로."**
- Outcome line: **"우리 개 체력나이, 두 살 어려졌어요."** (the app's 체력나이 chip is a ready-made marketing device — make it a shareable card)

**Runner dialect — Nike-coded, athletic identity, 러닝크루 energy.** Here the intense commercial instinct is *right*. Fast cuts, breathing, paws + shoes hitting pavement in sync, sunrise 한강.

- Recruit line: **"뛰던 길에서, 벌자."**
- Identity line: **"당신의 러닝에 심박수 하나 더."**
- Positioning: being a 댕런 러너is a *credential* (fitness-tested, certified, insured) — scarcity marketing, "상위 러너만 통과합니다" (mirrors 와요's top-10% acceptance flex).

### Content engine (product = studio)

The trust stack produces marketing automatically; almost zero content production cost:

1. **Dog-POV bodycam reels** — 15–30s runs, wind + panting + 한강. Owned format no competitor can copy without our hardware habit.
2. **Before/after** — dog bouncing off walls → post-run zen dog on the mat. The single most shareable dog format that sells the *outcome*.
3. **체력나이 카드** — auto-generated shareable card after each run (dog photo in the ring + fitness age + distance). Built into the app already; add a "share" button and it's the viral loop.
4. **러너 스토리** — runner dialect content doubling as recruitment ads.
5. **주간 리포트 캡처** — owners screenshot-share weekly recaps into 네이버 카페/카톡 gangs organically if the card is pretty enough.

### What we explicitly do NOT do in year one

Bus/subway ads, TV/YouTube pre-roll at scale, pamphlets, nationwide anything. Mass awareness before density burns money and creates zero-supply first impressions. (오늘의집/직방 ran TV *after* PMF.) Revisit at Series A.

## 3. 90-day launch playbook (single-neighborhood density)

**Launch 동 decision (week 1):** score 반포/잠원, 성수, 망원 on: dog density (KB: Seoul 113만 pet households), 한강공원 access, 러닝크루 presence, apartment community reachability. Gut lean: 반포/잠원 (한강 + high-spend 아파트 단지) or 성수 (dog culture + crew culture + media gravity). Pick ONE.

### Phase 0 — Setup (weeks 1–2)
- KIPRIS trademark search; naming go/no-go. Register Instagram + 네이버 플레이스 + 당근 비즈프로필.
- Recruit first 10 runners via 러닝크루 partnerships (2–3 crews in launch 동): pitch = "get paid to run + certification badge." Free coffee-chat info session after a crew run.
- Draft runner certification bar v1: pace test, dog-handling onboarding, 신원인증, insurance enrollment. The bar IS the brand.
- **Apply to 올해의 K-스타트업 (deadline Aug 31)** — see §4.

### Phase 1 — Supply + founding demand (weeks 3–6)
- Grow to 20–30 certified runners. Over-supply on purpose; owners must never see an empty map.
- **Founding member program:** 30–50 dogs in launch 동 get 2 free runs (콜드스타트 비용, ~₩1.5–2M). Recruit via: 당근 동네생활 posts, apartment 커뮤니티/부녀회, vet clinics + 강아지 유치원 + pet salons (poster/QR trade: they get free runs for their own dogs).
- **멍스타그램 seeding:** 10 micro dogfluencers (5k–50k followers, launch-동-local preferred) get free weekly runs for a month in exchange for posts. Their content = our ads.
- 네이버 카페 presence: genuine review-style posts in 강사모-type + 지역 카페 from real founding members (never astroturf — ask happy members to post, offer nothing but a thank-you; fake reviews in 카페 get sniffed out and are fatal).

### Phase 2 — The flywheel event (weeks 7–10)
- **댕런 크루 — every Saturday morning, 한강공원 (launch 동 section):** free group dog-run, branded race bibs (already in the app's visual language), finisher photo for every dog delivered to owners same-day, 체력나이 측정 booth. This one event simultaneously: recruits runners, converts owners, generates a week of content, and builds the local monopoly feel. Cost ~₩300–500k/event (bibs, banner, water, treats).
- Convert free founding members → paid **membership (주 2–3회, ₩199k–299k/mo 가설)**; single runs exist but are priced to push membership.
- Referral: 1 free run for both sides; apartment-단지 leaderboard if data allows.

### Phase 3 — Prove and measure (weeks 11–13)
- Targets to hit (these are the fundraising slide): **≥50 paying dogs, ≥60% M1 rebooking, ≥40% of bookings on membership, NPS from photo-delivery moment.** Same-runner rebooking rate is the trust metric investors will ask about.
- Retro: CAC by channel (expect 멍스타그램 + event ≪ any paid channel), supply utilization, incident count (must be zero — one incident in a 네이버 카페 kills a neighborhood).
- Decision gate: second 동 vs deepen.

**90-day budget (scrappy): ~₩6–9M** — free-run subsidies ₩2M, influencer gifting ₩1M, events ×6 ₩2–3M, print/QR materials ₩0.5M, small IG boost budget ₩1–2M. No paid channel above ₩2M until organic CAC is known.

### The narrative (deck ammo, all sourced)

1. **Market:** 삼정KPMG — Korean pet market 8.5조 (2022) → **21조 by 2032**. KB 2025: 5.91M pet households (26.7%), monthly spend ₩194k and rising, Seoul+경기 = 51.7% of pet households.
2. **Demand wedge:** 41.2% of owners rank regular exercise as a top wellness priority (KB 2025) — and it's the #1 guilt point of the busy urban owner. We sell absolution with GPS proof.
3. **Category creation:** first dog-running marketplace anywhere. Walking comps stalled at small rounds (도그메이트 ₩2.4B → acquired; 펫트너 ₩1.7B); capital in pet flows to health/commerce (핏펫 cumulative ~₩900억, 파우치 60억 Series A 2025, **페오펫 targeting 2026 KOSDAQ IPO** — category maturing to exits).
4. **Why we don't die like Wag:** subscription-led, premium-priced, density-first — Rover economics, not Wag economics.
5. **Moat:** running-specific certification + exertion-safety protocols + 체력나이 data layer → this is also the TIPS R&D framing (matching/safety algorithm, biometric-ish pet fitness data).
6. **Traction slide = §3's Phase 3 numbers.** Ten owners in one 동 rebooking the same runner weekly beats any TAM slide.

### Sequence recommendation

**Now–Aug:** K-스타트업 application (free option on 5억 + press) + launch Phase 0–1. **Sep–Dec:** run the 90-day playbook, apply Primer batch 29 / SparkLabs with early density data; decide incorporation path. **Jan–Mar 2027:** hit the 정부지원 window (예창패 or 초창패/청창사 depending on incorporation) with 90-day traction attached. **Mid-2027:** TIPS via operator (SparkLabs-type) once an accelerator check lands.
