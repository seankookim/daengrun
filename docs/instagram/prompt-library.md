## 0. READ THIS BEFORE GENERATING ANYTHING

### 0.1 TYPE IS PART OF THE LOOK — BOLD, TERSE, IN-FRAME

**Extremely concise, brand-bold display type is a core element of this campaign, not decoration.** The reference set's grammar includes giant volt/white statement type sitting inside the photograph. Rules of the type itself: **2–4 words maximum**, heavy Korean gothic (Black Han Sans register), volt #C6F542 or flash-white #F8F6F0 on the dark frames, oversized — type scaled to the architecture, not to a caption. One statement per frame. If a line needs a second clause, it isn't the line.

**Two production routes — pick per frame, judged only by the result:**

1. **Generated in-frame (ChatGPT/GPT-4o image):** the preferred route when type should interact with the scene (perspective on wet asphalt, wrapped on a singlet, glowing off a wall). Put the exact Korean string in quotes with placement and treatment: `with the Korean text "산책 말고, 러닝" set enormous in heavy black Korean gothic sans, volt yellow-green, lower third, slight perspective matching the road surface`. Proofread every glyph at 100% zoom — one wrong 받침 in a hero line becomes a screenshot. MJ/Imagen/Flux still break Hangul: for those tools, generate the clean plate and use route 2.
2. **Set in post (Figma/Photoshop, real Black Han Sans):** for flat lockup-style placements, and whenever route 1's letterforms drift. Each entry's **타이포 스펙** block (copy, position, scale ratio, hex, weight) applies to BOTH routes — it is the type spec, not a post-only instruction.

**Vector-only, any tool (correctness, not taste):** the 도그스하이 wordmark lockup (its exact letterforms are the logo — generators approximate, and drift breaks the mark) · **any real number** — payout ₩ figures, 체력나이 decimals, dates, distances (a hallucinated digit is an honesty failure, not a typo) · app-UI screens/chips · bib numbers · body-length copy (display type is 2–4 words; anything longer is caption territory and goes in post or in the caption itself).

Operational notes:

1. Prompts in this library ship with `no text, no lettering, no logos, no watermarks, no signage` in the negative block **as the clean-plate baseline** — when you want route-1 in-frame type, DELETE that line and append the quoted string instruction instead. Never leave both.
2. Even on clean plates, reserve the type zone at generation time by asking for compositional negative space (sky, water, asphalt, deep shadow) in the stated corner. Type scaled to the architecture needs room.
3. Brand rule that survives: **900 weight is for numbers and titles only.** Body/caption lines sit at 500–700 in a standard Korean sans.
4. Incidental/invented environmental text (fake shop signs, fake Latin logos on apparel) is still banned everywhere — the only text in frame is OUR statement.

### 0.2 HONESTY GATE — which images may be AI at all

Absolute rules from the brand honesty standard, translated into generation policy:

| Surface | AI allowed? | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Brand-world / concept / platform posts (TWO HEARTS, STAIR, POV FLIP, COIN RUN, category declaration, breed portraits, mascot) | **Yes** | Caption must read as concept/brand-world or coming-soon. No implied customer, no implied completed run. |
| Pre-launch service scenes (a runner running a dog) | **Yes, with caption disclosure** | Caption must frame it as 컨셉/예고 — e.g. "곧 시작합니다" — never as a delivered service. |
| Event teasers before the event exists | **Yes, labeled 예고** | The moment the real Saturday event runs, **replace with real photography.** AI event imagery has a hard expiry date. |
| Customer dogs, testimonials, before/after of a named dog | **NO** | We have zero customers. No AI-generated "customer." Ever. |
| Finisher photos, cert-day cohort photos, crew takeover | **NO once real** | Concept plate only, pre-event, labeled. Real photos the day they exist. |
| GPS trails, bodycam frames, app screenshots, 체력나이 cards with numbers | **NO** | Product surfaces come from the real app or a vector mockup labeled as UI mockup. Never a generated screen. |
| Earnings figures, health/fitness claims | **NO** | Numbers come from the honest-math table only. 체력나이 is our metric; lifespan/life-expectancy framing is banned. |

Practical consequence for this library: **entries marked `GATE: CONCEPT-ONLY` must carry a concept/예고 caption.** Entries marked `GATE: REPLACE-WHEN-REAL` are stopgaps with a defined retirement.

### 0.3 The one-photographer rule

The grid must read as if a single photographer with a single camera body and a two-lens kit shot everything at the same two hours of the day. That is what §1 (style block) enforces. Do not freestyle lighting or grade per post — swap subject and angle, keep the light and the grade locked.

## 1.0 REFERENCE DNA — 왜 이 룩인가 (읽고 시작할 것)

**v1 (dawn-pastel) → v2 (night flash). Pivot date 2026-08-04, founder reference set (7 images).**

v1 of this library shot the whole account at dawn on the Han River: low sun, luminous
haze, teal-olive pastel grade, long soft shadows. It was coherent and it was safe, and
it read as *lifestyle*. The founder's reference set — seven frames of modern running-
campaign photography — is a different grammar entirely: **direct on-camera hard flash
at night**, subject crisp and slightly blown against a background that has fallen to
near-black, wet asphalt behaving like a mirror, rain droplets sitting on the front
element as fat soft orbs, rear-curtain flash drag smearing limbs and tail-lights while
the torso stays frozen, brutalist concrete sets — bridge undersides, ribbed tunnels,
underpasses, traffic-flanked streets, striped construction barriers. Crews photographed
like a team, mid-effort and mid-laugh, one volt accent burning out of the dark.

Why we took it: the category we are claiming is **running**, not walking. Dawn light says
"pleasant morning with a pet." Flash-at-night says "this is training, and it happened."
The grammar is also honest-cheap — it is exactly what a real run at 9pm in 반포 looks like
when someone shoots it with a flash, which means the day we replace these plates with
real photography (see §0.2 gates, §5.5), the real photos will match the AI plates instead
of exposing them. That continuity is the whole argument.

**Ours, not in the references:** the dog. Every reference frame is human-only. Our twist is
a dog at genuine full athletic stride, integrated as a teammate — volt bandana or bib,
leash geometry visible, running like it belongs on the start line. Flash plus animal eyes
produces tapetum eyeshine (the glowing-eye look of a bad night snapshot), which reads as
horror-adjacent and instantly cheap. **Every prompt with a dog facing camera carries
natural-eye handling in the positive AND the negative** — this is not optional and it is
the single most common re-roll cause in v2.

What did NOT change: the honesty gates (§0.2), the Korean-text rules (§0.1), the volt
`#C6F542` single-accent discipline, the one-photographer rule, and the copy bank. We
changed the light, not the ethics.

**Exceptions, deliberate and enumerated** (a future operator should not "fix" these):
IMG-D3 and the mascot frames (IMG-H1/IMG-H2) are **flat cream/clay graphic tiles**, not
photographs — they are the light counterweight that makes the dark tiles read as a grid
rather than a smear (§5.1). IMG-E3 (지친 개) is **ambient-only night interior, no flash** —
you do not pop a flash at a sleeping dog, and the concept is stillness. IMG-G1 is **pre-
sunrise blue-black dawn with flash**, because its overlay copy says 토요일 아침 and the
event genuinely is a morning run; ambient stays deeply underexposed, so it still belongs
to the T1 family.

### v3 — 두 번째 레퍼런스 세트 (16장): 하나의 룩에서 하나의 시스템으로

**Pivot date 2026-08-04 (same day, second delivery). Founder reference set 2: 16 frames of
sports-brand campaign work.**

The first set defined a *look*. The second set defines a *system*: sixteen frames that are
obviously one campaign and are **not one photographic treatment.** Night flash sits beside
golden zoom-blur, beside impressionist ghost-blur, beside a solarised duotone poster,
beside a fisheye where one object is made monumental, beside a near-empty studio, beside a
cold overcast landscape, beside a page of pure type with no photograph at all. What holds
them together is not the light — it is the **attitude**: one accent colour, real athletic
bodies, candid documentary honesty, disciplined colour, and type that speaks like a person
rather than a brand.

So v3 restructures §1 into **base DNA + eight treatment modules (T1–T8)**. T1 is the v2
night-flash grammar, unchanged, and stays the account's home base — roughly half of what we
publish. The other seven are what turn a consistent feed into a campaign.

**Consequence for the v2 rule set:** the blanket ban on golden-hour warmth was a T1 rule
written when T1 was the only treatment. **It is now scoped to T1** (and to T3/T4/T7, which
have their own colour logic). T2 exists precisely for warm low sun and lens flare; T6 is
soft even studio light; T7 is flat overcast; L2 is harsh summer daylight because the concept
is heat. Warm light is no longer a violation — **warm light inside a T1 frame is.**

**§0.3의 one-photographer rule은 v3에서 이렇게 읽는다:** 한 명의 사진가가 아니라 **한 명의 아트 디렉터**.
같은 사람이 여덟 개의 트리트먼트를 골라 쓴 것으로 읽혀야 하고, 그 통일성은 광질이 아니라 **볼트 한 점 ·
진짜 주법 · 타입 시스템 · 그레인**이 만든다. 프레임 안에서 트리트먼트를 섞는 것은 여전히 금지 —
한 프레임은 하나의 트리트먼트에 완전히 커밋한다. 섞이는 순간 필터로 보인다.

## 1. STYLE SYSTEM — BASE DNA + 8 TREATMENTS (paste TWO blocks into every prompt)

v2 shipped one look. v3 ships a system. **Every prompt carries exactly two blocks appended
at the end: §1.1 BASE DNA (always, verbatim) + one treatment module from §1.2 (T1–T8).**

- **Never stack two treatments in one frame.** A frame that is half flash and half ghost-blur
  reads as a mistake. Commit.
- **Never omit the base DNA.** It is the thing making eight treatments one account.
- Each entry in §4 declares its treatment on the `TREATMENT:` line. Follow it; if you want a
  different treatment for a concept, that is a new variation with a new ID, not a re-light.

### 1.1 BASE DNA (paste into EVERY prompt, all treatments)

```
BASE DNA — DOGS HIGH:
ACCENT: exactly one saturated accent in the frame — volt yellow-green #C6F542 —
carried by a bandana, bib, singlet, leash or webbing. Never on the animal's own
coat, never two accents. Everything else is restrained.
COLOUR: either crushed deep blacks at #0F1D13, or ONE disciplined colour field (a
single saturated channel, a muted overcast field, a clean seamless). Never a busy
multi-hue frame, never HDR, never an orange-teal blockbuster grade.
BODIES: genuine full athletic stride, correct running form, mid-air moments prized.
Dogs move like teammates, not pets — no sitting pretty, no begging, no cuteness,
no costume. Leash geometry, when present, is physically correct and taut.
ENERGY: candid documentary — mid-effort, mid-laugh, open mouths breathing, sweat,
breath vapor. Nobody performs for the camera unless the gesture is real: a peace
sign thrown mid-stride is real, a stock smile is not.
TEXTURE: 35mm editorial grain, honest optical imperfection, no plastic skin, no
digital over-cleanliness, no beauty retouching.
TYPE SPACE: at most one statement of type per frame; hold clean negative space for
it where the entry's 타이포 스펙 says, scaled to the architecture.
ANIMAL EYES (mandatory in every treatment): any dog toward the lens has natural
dark wet eyes with one small specular catchlight — no eye glow, no tapetum
reflection, no retroreflective green or white eyeshine, no glowing pupils.
```

*(Clean-plate baseline: `NO TEXT ANYWHERE IN THE IMAGE.` is appended by the §2 negative. For
route-1 in-frame type, delete that line and append the quoted Korean string per §0.1.)*

### 1.2 TREATMENT MODULES

#### T1 — NIGHT FLASH *(home base · ~half the library)*
**Use for:** the default. Service scenes, crews, recruitment, product atoms, anything that must
read as "this happened at 9pm on a wet street."

```
TREATMENT T1 — NIGHT FLASH:
Direct hard on-camera flash at night: the subject crisp and a half-stop hot against
a deeply underexposed urban background, hard specular highlights on wet skin, wet
fur and wet asphalt, visible flash falloff vignette dropping the frame edges to
black. Ambient practicals carry the rest — car headlights, motorcycle lamps,
tungsten street glow, red tail-light wash bleeding across wet ground.
ATMOSPHERE: rain or post-rain. Water droplets ON THE LENS as large soft
out-of-focus orbs, mirror-specular wet asphalt, breath vapor, steam off shoulders.
MOTION: slow-shutter flash drag on rear-curtain sync — face and torso frozen by the
flash while limbs, lights and background smear into streaks; or panning blur with
only the lead subject semi-sharp.
SET: brutalist concrete at night — bridge undersides, ribbed tunnels, spiral-ceiling
underpasses, streets with layered traffic, orange-and-white striped construction
barriers as found graphics. Wet ground throughout.
GRADE: crushed blacks #0F1D13, high contrast, cool shadows, flash-white rolloff into
cream #F8F6F0, NO golden-hour warmth in this treatment, no HDR, no lifted blacks.
```
**MJ short:** `flash documentary night running, direct hard on-camera flash, deeply underexposed night city, wet asphalt speculars, rain droplets on lens, rear-curtain flash drag streaks, brutalist underpass, headlight and tail-light practicals, crushed blacks, volt green accent, 35mm grain`

#### T2 — GOLDEN ZOOM-BLUR
**Use for:** euphoria, the runner's-high beat, recruitment energy. The one warm treatment.

```
TREATMENT T2 — GOLDEN ZOOM-BLUR:
Low late-day sun raking in under bridge structures, overpasses and elevated
roadways, the sun in or just outside the frame throwing warm flare, veiling glare
and haze straight into the lens. Warm filmic grade — amber and gold highlights,
soft warm-brown shadows, halation blooming off every bright edge, sunlit dust in
the air.
MOTION: in-camera zoom burst / radial blur — every edge in the frame streaks
outward from the centre in radial lines while the centred subject holds sharp at
the core. Motion drawn as light rather than as smear. Backlit subject rimmed and
half-dissolved by flare, shot wide open into the sun.
NOTE: the "no golden-hour warmth" rule belongs to T1 only. Do not paste T1 grade
language into a T2 prompt.
```
**MJ short:** `warm golden hour under a bridge, sun flare into lens, zoom burst radial blur streaking outward, sharp centred runner, halation, filmic warm grade, dust in light, volt green accent, 35mm grain`

#### T3 — GHOST BLUR
**Use for:** platform/emotional frames — above all 두 개의 심장, where runner and dog ghost into
one motion mass. Quiet, not loud.

```
TREATMENT T3 — GHOST BLUR (impressionist long exposure):
Long exposure, no flash. Whole bodies ghost and double-expose into transparent
streaks, limbs multiplying, edges dissolving into the background. NOTHING in the
frame is fully sharp — if one element resolves crisply the treatment has failed.
COLOUR: pastel field — blue-hour lavender and steel, fog white, pale water, muted
dusk rose. Low saturation, low contrast, luminous rather than dark.
MOOD: painterly, quiet, emotional, almost abstract. The subject reads as movement
and gesture, not as an identifiable person or breed.
CAMERA: 1/2s to 2s, handheld or panned, camera moving with the subject, tripod
never.
```

### 1.4 MIX RULES (what keeps it a campaign)

- **T1 ≈ 50%** of published photographic frames. It is the home base; everything else is
  seasoning. A feed that is 8 treatments in equal parts reads as an agency portfolio.
- **T4 max 1 per 9-grid.** T8 max 2 per 9-grid, and never adjacent.
- **Any 9-grid draws from ≥ 4 families** (§5.1). Fewer and it reads as a filter.
- **Volt appears in every frame, exactly once** — that and the type system are the only
  through-lines you get. Protect them.
- **Never mix two LIGHT treatments inside one frame.** Adjacent tiles may contrast hard; a
  single frame may not hedge.
- **Light treatments vs render treatments.** T1 / T2 / T3 / T6 / T7 define the **light** and are
  mutually exclusive — exactly one per frame, never two. T4 (duotone), T5 (fisheye monument)
  and the §1.3 motifs define **geometry and render**; they sit on top of a light treatment and,
  unless the entry says otherwise, inherit **T1**. So `T5 on T1` is normal and legal (IMG-I1,
  IMG-I2), `T4 on T1` is normal (IMG-J1), and `T2 on T3` is not a thing.

**Grade-token cheat sheet (v3):** `#0F1D13` = near-black in T1/T4, forest field in T8, and is
simply absent from T2/T3/T6/T7 — do not force it into a pastel or overcast frame. `#F8F6F0` =
flash highlight (T1), cream field (T6/T8), paper white (T7). `#EDE8DA` warm clay survives in
fur/skin midtones and as a graphic-tile ground. Tang `#FF5C3D` remains retired as a styling
accent — practical reds (tail-lights) do that job for free.

### 2.1 Dog-anatomy QC checklist (reject before it reaches the grid)

Generators fail dogs in predictable ways. Reject and re-roll if you see: 5 legs or a phantom rear leg in motion blur · paw pads facing the wrong direction · leash that does not physically connect to collar and hand · double tail · muzzle length wrong for the stated breed (허스키 rendered as 말라뮤트, 진도믹스 rendered as 시바) · 웰시코기 given long legs · human hand with 6 fingers on the leash · dog's eyes rendered with human sclera. **v2 addition — reject on sight: glowing/reflective dog eyes (tapetum eyeshine).** Flash plus an animal facing the lens is the #1 failure mode of this look; the fix is prompt-side (natural-eye clause in positive + eyeshine negatives) and, failing that, a 5-minute paint-out of the pupils in post — never a shipped glow. Also reject: flash falloff that clips to pure #000 with no shadow detail at all, and a "flash drag" that blurred the *face* instead of the limbs. Budget **8–12 generations per final image** in v2 (up from 6–10 — the eyes cost the difference); this is the single largest time cost in the library.

## 3. PER-GENERATOR ADAPTATION NOTES

> These are **starting suggestions, not verified settings.** Model versions and parameter names change frequently — verify each flag against the tool's current documentation before a production run.

### 3.0 The four T1 techniques, phrased per tool

Everything below is a variation on getting four things out of a generator. Learn these
four phrasings and the rest is subject matter.

| Technique | Phrasing that works | Fails as |
|---|---|---|
| **Hard on-camera flash** | `direct on-camera flash at night, harsh frontal flash, subject brightly lit and slightly overexposed against a deeply underexposed near-black background, hard specular highlights, flash falloff vignette at the frame edges` | ask for "night photo" alone and you get ambient city glow — soft, even, and wrong |
| **Flash drag (rear-curtain)** | `slow shutter 1/15s with rear-curtain sync flash: the torso and face frozen sharp by the flash while the limbs, the traffic lights and the background smear into directional motion streaks` — always say **what stays sharp** first | say "motion blur" alone and the face blurs; the frame is then unusable |
| **Rain on the lens** | `water droplets on the front lens element, rendered as large soft out-of-focus circular orbs across the frame, refracting the streetlights behind them` | "rain" alone gives falling-rain streaks; "bokeh" alone gives background balls. Say **on the lens** |
| **No eyeshine** | positive: `natural dark wet dog eyes with a small specular catchlight, no eye glow` + negative: the §2 EYE HANDLING list | omit it and flash + animal = glowing green retinas, every time |

### 3.0b The v3 treatment techniques, phrased per tool

| Technique | Phrasing that works | Notes / failure |
|---|---|---|
| **T2 zoom burst** | `in-camera zoom burst: every edge streaking outward from the centre in radial lines while the centred subject stays sharp` — say **radial/outward from centre**, and name what stays sharp | "motion blur" gives directional smear, not radial. MJ is the weakest here; Flux and Imagen honour "radial zoom blur" much better |
| **T2 flare** | `shooting into a low sun, heavy veiling glare, warm halation blooming off every highlight, sunlit dust` | GPT-4o will clean the flare away — instruct "keep the lens flare and the haze" explicitly |
| **T3 ghosting** | `1 second exposure, the bodies ghosted and double-exposed into transparent overlapping streaks, nothing in the frame fully sharp` | every generator wants to resolve a sharp face — repeat "nothing sharp" twice, and reject any roll with a readable face |
| **T4 duotone** | `duotone: the entire image reduced to a single saturated volt yellow-green channel against solid black, posterised, screen-printed poster, no other colour` | asking for "green filter" gives a tinted photo. Say **duotone / posterised / screen print**. Flux is best; MJ needs `--s 250` here (the one place high stylize helps) |
| **T5 fisheye** | `circular fisheye 8mm from ground level, horizon strongly curved, buildings bending inward at the edges, the dog enormous and close to the lens` | the risk is anatomy: distortion + dog = extra legs. Keep every anatomy negative and budget extra rolls |
| **T6 seamless** | `full-frame warm grey seamless studio backdrop, no floor line, no seam, soft even light, subject small and low in frame` | generators add a horizon or a prop; say "no horizon line, no props" outright. Best tool: Imagen |
| **T7 overcast scale** | `flat shadowless overcast winter light, fog, the runner and dog very small and far away in a wide frame` | "cinematic" is poison here — it adds contrast and drama. Ask for *catalogue* / *documentary* |
| **T8 flat field** | do not generate a photograph at all — build in Figma (route 2), or GPT-4o route 1 for ≤4-word statements: `a flat solid volt yellow-green field, no image, with the Korean text "…" set enormous in heavy black Korean gothic sans, centred, filling most of the frame` | MJ/Flux/Imagen will not set Hangul correctly. Never ship an unproofed glyph |

### Midjourney
- Base flags: `--ar 4:5` (feed) / `--ar 9:16` (reel/story) / `--ar 1:1` (grid tile) · `--style raw` on every prompt — **more important in v2 than v1.** Default MJ beautifies night into soft cinematic ambient glow and will not give you an ugly-honest direct flash; raw is what keeps the flash hard and the falloff steep.
- `--s 50` to `--s 150` (lower than v1's 100–250). High stylize is what invents neon-purple night grades and blooming halos — the two failure looks closest to ours.
- `--chaos 0` for locked-grade grid posts; `--chaos 10–20` only when exploring an angle.
- Consistency: build ONE approved hero frame, then carry it with `--sref <url>` at `--sw 80–120` across the library. In v2 the sref is doing more work than in v1 — flash quality and falloff depth are the hardest things to describe and the easiest to reference. `--cref <url> --cw 40–70` for a recurring dog.
- Put subject + action in the first 15 words; then the flash clause; then the set; style block last, compressed (§1 MJ SHORT). **The word "flash" must appear before word 25** or MJ will light the frame with practicals only.
- `--no` list per §2, plus the v2 night offenders: `--no text, logo, watermark, cartoon, 3d render, extra limbs, deformed paws, stock photo, retractable leash, eyeshine, glowing eyes, neon, cyberpunk, purple haze, sunset`.
- Known MJ v2 weaknesses: it renders lens droplets as generic background bokeh (accept it or add them in post), and it loves to put a second colour accent in — QC every frame for a stray magenta or cyan practical. Avoid `--weird`.

### DALL·E 3 / GPT-4o image
- No aspect flags — request the ratio in words: "Vertical 4:5 portrait format." 9:16 often arrives closer to 2:3, so **compose with headroom and crop down**.
- It rewrites your prompt and it **relights toward pleasant**. Counter with a literal instruction at the top: "Render exactly the following scene. Do not add elements, do not stylize, do not brighten the background — the background must stay very dark."
- Weakest of the four at hard flash: it tends to give a well-exposed night street with an evenly lit subject. Push it with exposure language it understands — "as if shot at ISO 400, f/8, with a camera-mounted flash as the only light on the subject; everything the flash does not reach is nearly black."
- Motion: it cannot do a convincing flash drag. Ask for the frozen version and treat GPT-4o output as **composition sketches and layout tests**, then rebuild the winner in Flux or MJ.
- It will refuse or soften prompts containing brand names. **Never write any sportswear or competitor brand name in a prompt** — §1 describes the aesthetic without naming anyone, which is the whole point of having a written style system. Never name a real athlete or celebrity (§5.4).
- **Korean text: the one generator allowed to render it** (§0.1 exception). Short display headlines (≤6 words) come out clean most of the time. Exact string in quotes, "heavy black Korean gothic sans, tight tracking," state position and colour in plain words. Proofread every glyph at full zoom. Wordmark, numbers, body copy stay post-composited vector.
- Hex codes are only loosely honored. Add the plain-language colour name alongside every hex ("volt yellow-green #C6F542"), and for v2 add value words too ("near-black background", "blown-out white highlight").

### Imagen (Google, Imagen 3/4 family via Vertex or Gemini)
- Best-in-class at photographic realism and fine texture (wet fur, sweat, asphalt grain, specular water) — **the strongest default for v2's texture-heavy frames.**
- Use the dedicated `negativePrompt` field with the full §2 list including the new EYE HANDLING and NIGHT-LOOK blocks. Paste whole. This is the only tool where the eyeshine negatives reliably do the work by themselves.
- `aspectRatio` `4:5` / `9:16` / `1:1` where supported; otherwise `3:4` and crop.
- Photography vocabulary is honored well — **keep the exposure triplet in every v2 prompt** ("1/15s at f/5.6, ISO 400, on-camera flash"). Imagen respects shutter values, which is how you buy the flash drag.
- `personGeneration` policy may block or alter clear faces. Most of this library is intentionally faceless (backs, silhouettes, cropped-at-jaw, ground-level) — a brand choice and a reliability choice. In v2 the flash falloff gives a third option: **faces present but half-swallowed by darkness.**

### Flux (Flux 1.1 pro / dev)
- Best prose adherence of the four; write the primary prompt as flowing sentences. The 150–200 word v2 prompts here are written for Flux first and trimmed for MJ.
- `guidance_scale` **2.0–3.0 for v2** (v1 used 2.5–3.5). Higher guidance at night blows the flash highlight to a flat white blob and plasticizes wet skin. `steps` 28–40. Fix `seed` once a frame is approved.
- Strongest tool for the two things v2 needs most: **specular wet surfaces** and **legible in-image graphic shapes** (the ECG line, the coin discs, barrier stripes).
- Handles "droplets on the lens" better than MJ if you describe the optics literally ("out-of-focus water beads clinging to the front element, refracting the streetlights").
- Distilled guidance means negatives are weak — express exclusions positively ("natural dark eyes with a small catchlight", "background falls to black", "clean unmarked apparel").
- For ECG-line frames, generate the plate clean and add the line as **vector in post**. Same for coins (§D) — at night, prompted coins now collide with lens-droplet bokeh and read as flare.

### Universal
- Generate at max native resolution, upscale once, never twice. Instagram re-compresses hard; over-sharpened uploads look worse than soft ones. **v2 caution:** crushed blacks + IG compression = banding in large dark areas. Keep a little noise in the shadows (the §1 grain clause is load-bearing, not decorative) and check the upload on a phone at low brightness before it goes live.
- Keep a `seed` / `job id` log next to each approved frame so a variation can be re-pulled months later.
- **Every frame gets a human QC pass against §2.1 before it enters the grid** — in v2 that pass starts with the dog's eyes.

The platform frames. These carry the campaign; their placement in the launch 9-cut is fixed by `campaign-concepts.md` §1.5 (see §5.1 below — do not re-sequence). The two parallel ECG lines are the signature: **volt #C6F542 = 개, white #F8F6F0 = 사람**, running horizontally across the frame, diverging in rhythm and syncing at the moment of stride contact.

> **Production preference:** generate the photographic plate clean, add the two ECG lines as **vector in post-production** (exact hex, exact stroke, exact sync point). Prompt-generated ECG lines are a fallback for concept exploration only — every generator will render a medically wrong or wobbling trace.

## B. 체력나이 STAIR — 계단 (2 images)

Seed idea 1. A dog climbs Han River stone stairs; the **체력나이 number counts DOWN** step by step (7.4 → 7.1 → 6.8 → 6.4). The number is 100% vector overlay, animated in post.

> **단위 규칙 (ECD 정정):** 체력나이는 **소수점 한 자리**로만 표기하고 **"살"/"세" 단위를 붙이지 않는다.** 정수 + 살은 실제 나이·수명으로 읽히고, 그 순간 우리가 금지한 건강 주장이 된다. 표기는 `체력나이 6.4` 하나로 통일 — `campaign-concepts.md` C1, `reel-scripts.md` R4, 하이라이트 커버까지 동일.

> ⛔ **HARD RULE: 체력나이 is a fitness-age metric, never life expectancy.** Never write or imply 수명, 오래 산다, +N년, longevity. The number goes down because *fitness age* improves. No before/after health claim, no vet-sounding certainty. Copy stays in the register of "지표가 좋아진다," not "더 오래 산다."
