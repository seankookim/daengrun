## 0. What changed and why

`account-launch-plan.md` §1.3 builds the entire launch on nine tiles keyed to `IMG-A1`–`IMG-A4`,
`IMG-B1`, `IMG-C2`, `IMG-D1` — the prompt-library IDs. **Those assets were never produced.** What was
produced is the 67-file set in `/Users/sean/Desktop/post`: a coral-red / black / white poster system
with a violet GPS route trace and a running-dog glyph.

So the grid device survives and its contents change. Everything else in the launch plan — bio rules,
highlight architecture, hashtag sets, the no-tag teaser week, the metrics, the batch schedule — stands
as written and is not restated here.

> ⚠ **Open for Sean:** the produced assets do not contain the volt/white **pulse rail**
> (`campaign-concepts.md` §1.4). They use a **violet route trace with a PACE/KM readout** instead. I
> built this campaign on the trace because it is what exists *and* because it maps to a real product
> surface — the app genuinely draws that line. But the pulse rail is written as canon, so this is a
> divergence to rule on, not a preference I get to settle.
